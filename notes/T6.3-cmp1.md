## Summary

CMP1 = CMP0 with the 3-band compressor's three pages on the MIXER view. The pages Compressor Low, Mid and High
(1/7 to 3/7) replace the stock Compressor page; each has its band's THR, ATK, REL, MUP, RAT and crossover (none on
HIGH), the stock SCS, MIX and VOL, a CMP/LOW|MID|HI box and **that band's own gain-reduction bar**. The band knobs
show on screen but **do not reach the DSP yet** (that is CMP2's frame tail): the hidden stock k 60..64 still drive
all three bands, as on CMP0. The DSP is CMP0's plus the CMP0 review's guard fixes; on normal material its output is
CMP0's sample for sample.

## 3. DSP side [verified, `out/T6.3/CMP1-dsp/`]

### 3.1 What changed against CMP0 (the CMP0 review's findings)

From the review's runs (`out/T6.3/review-safety3`), CMP0's guard reset cleared only filters and envelopes; a NaN in
the crossover coefficients, detector coefficients, a band's derived times/ratio/threshold/makeup target, the MIX
targets or the dry-crossfade position tripped the guard every block: silence until power-off. CMP1 (`fx/mbcomp.c`,
`src/cmp_glue.c`):

1. **Complete restart** (`mb_restart`): everything is derived again from the knobs through NaN-safe clamps (exponent
   or two-sided range tests: on the SHARC a NaN compares greater than everything, so C-style clamps are not
   NaN-safe there [verified from selcc's output]); a scrub re-derives one group of derived values per block (all
   every 8 blocks; on normal material it writes the same values); a range test on the smoothers restarts the state
   before the block; the glue compares each stock knob with the compressor's own knob word and re-sends it when
   they differ, sets the table pointer every call, and re-initialises quietly on a corrupted magic; the signal
   guard is 2^20 (+120 dBFS), down from 2^30.
2. **Quiet return**: after a restart both MIX gains start from 0 (the stock one-poles, 4.2 ms), every band attacks
   instantly for 48 blocks (32 ms), and the glue seeds each band's envelope with the previous block's gain reduction
   when deeper.
3. **verify_cmp.py (b)** also compares the dry-crossfade branch and the restart paths (the same fault injected on
   SHARC and Mac).

CMP1b (the CMP1 review, section 9) added, in `fx/mbcomp.c` (#15, #16) and `src/cmp_glue.c`:

4. **Quiet re-init on any bad magic, 0 included**: `cmp_init`, instant attack on every band for 48 blocks (all
   bands marked dirty), both envelopes of each band seeded from the last block's gain reduction, the MIX gains from
   0, the count in `reinits` (the first build's `pad` word: the layout is unchanged). The first build took a zero
   magic for the cold start (no instant attack, no seed, not counted) and seeded no other bad magic either.
5. **The range test before the block covers the knob-derived values** the scrub reaches only every 8 blocks: each
   band's time coefficients in [0, 1], each paired with its exact complement (`ca1 == 1 - ca`; `mb_targets` stores
   `c` and `1.0f - c`, so one corrupted word breaks the pair), 1/ratio in [0.04, 1], makeup target in [0, 4],
   threshold in [-10, 0] log2; the MIX targets in [0, 1]; the crossover targets (30 Hz..1 kHz, 1..16 kHz) and
   coefficients ([0, 1]). The detector coefficients are derived every block.
6. **Envelope bound -32 log2** (-193 dB; first build -64), **guard 2^16** (+96 dBFS; first build 2^20), and the
   seed clamped to -16 log2 (-96 dB; first build -60), inside the bound so that a seed cannot restart the state
   again.

Deriving everything every block instead was built and measured too (`456ee820`): +3,514 instructions per block over
the first build, against +449 for these tests [verified, `out/T6.3/CMP1-dsp/probes/cmp1b-variants/`]. The owner's
policy (a heavy-load design is not wanted; rare edge cases are acceptable) decided it.

A new selcc miscompile was found on the way (inlining `mb_state_expo` made `mb_run` a leaf whose spill slot for the
loop count was reused inside a hardware loop: loops ran 28 times instead of 32); `mb_state_expo` is non-static, so
`mb_run` compiles as in CMP0. Repro and a one-pattern scan in `out/T6.3/CMP1-dsp/probes/selcc-leaf-spill-miscompile/`:
0 hits in CMP0, FTD1, FLG1, SE01 and the final CMP1 [verified run; the scan's coverage is one pattern, inferred
scope].

## 7. Limits, and decisions for the owner

1. **The compressor's sound cannot be set from the panel on CMP1.** The band knobs do not reach the DSP (CMP2), and
   the stock THR/ATK/REL/MUP/RAT (k 60..64) that still drive all three bands are on no page. They follow the kit
   (a kit set up on CMP0 or GT01 keeps its values), MIDI CC 111 THR, 112 ATK, 113 REL, 114 MUP, 115 RAT (NRPN
   272-276) [verified rows in `out/T6.5/tables/GT01-warm.json`; CMP1's stock rows are GT01's, build check], and
   possibly the Overbridge plugin [inferred, not tested]. MIX, SCS and VOL work from every band page [verified].
2. **The band knobs read 0 after a cold boot** (THR 0, RAT 1.00, MUP 0.0dB, crossovers 30Hz and 1.0k), not their
   defaults [verified in both warm snapshots; on the unit inferred]. The defaults appear after CLEAR PAGE (no key
   path in the MIXER view, see 4) or, by the row-default mechanism, a new kit [inferred, `FUN_400e7140`]. Band
   values are not saved (k 70..86 are outside the stock storage) [verified]. Cosmetic until CMP2.
3. **The RAT dial glyph is hard to read**: over 0..8 the borrowed stock dial (row 0x99's) takes 6 distinct images
   (1/2, 3/4 and 5/6 look the same) and its pointer stays near the bottom left [verified pixel count]. The text
   while turning is the readable value. The owner asked for this placeholder glyph; a better one needs a display
   entry copied at run time, as fx-echo does.
4. **Page copy/clear/paste is not reachable from the keys in the MIXER view**, on GT01's stock Compressor page
   either [verified on both]; proved by calling the OS's own functions.
5. **The stock Compressor page leaves the list**, and with it the deepest-band bar; the Overbridge GR value
   (half-word 11, the deepest band) is unchanged [verified (h): CMP1 writes the reply as CMP0].
6. **End to end, the band meters are a unit test**: the emulator drew faked half-words; sharcvm showed the DSP
   writing them (CMP0 verify (b), CMP1 (h)); the SPI byte order the ColdFire reads has only been seen on the unit
   for half-word 11 (stock, and dnfw on 1.11) [inferred for 8/9/10].
7. **Untested**: a real Outbox 8 (forced only); the Overbridge plugin with the band rows' uids 0x400+ (T6.4 6.1 #9);
   project save and load on the +Drive (the emulator's eMMC is empty: SAVE ERROR, as in T6.5 4.9; kit storage was
   checked on the stock save/load code).
8. **DSP load**: the worst tested block is est. 76.04 % (first build 75.95 %), 0.24 points over CMP0's [inferred
   calibration]. The owner judges by ear; FLASH-CMP1.md has a short stress test.
9. **DSP residuals, reported not fixed** [verified, `inject.json`]: a finite wrong value written straight into a
   gain target (`t_mw`/`t_md` 2..100, `t_mk` 5, `rinv` 100) makes the one block before the range test loud, up to
   the master's −3.19 dBFS ceiling (17 of 11,492 cases); the restart then returns quietly. Nothing is known to write
   those words (the store census shows only planned ranges). A finite ±100 input sample (below the 2^20 guard)
   passes as a +40 dB peak clipped by the wet clip, as any compressor would.
10. **The new selcc miscompile** (3.1) is avoided, not fixed; worth reporting to selache with the earlier ones.
11. **Edited for reproducibility**: `scripts/t50b_build.py`, `t65_build.py`, `t53_build.py` (core and param-rows 0.1
    from the pinned commit; t50b/t65 gained `--out`); 2 of the 26 elekloader tests adapted. `trip_loudness.py`
    recompiled the offsets program in `out/T6.3/CMP0-dsp/build/` (the CMP0 image and its evidence are unchanged).
12. **The compressor's code slot is nearly full** (CMP1b): `t38_code3` has 406 bytes left of `0x308400-0x310000`
    (the range test of the derived values took 1,488). CMP2's frame-tail code will need room: grow the slot or move
    code (`t38_code2`'s slot has 8,308 bytes left) [verified, build.json].
13. **The 2^16 guard** assumes no real bus level reaches +96 dBFS [inferred: not measured; 16 tracks at full scale
    sum to +24 dBFS, and the Mac tests feed peaks of +36 dBFS without a trip]. Above it a block would be silent and
    the state restarted (as with any bad value).
14. **Not touched**: CMP0's main-only container `out/T6.3/build/CMP0-main.syx` (version `CMP0`, stock DSP) has the
    pattern of finding 9; it is CMP0's evidence, left for the main session. elekloader's numbered claims compare

    `0x10-0x15`), so a mod that wrote half-word numbers there would not be compared correctly.
