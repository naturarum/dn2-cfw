# T6.3: CMP2E, breadcrumbs, a heartbeat and a probe switch (where the DSP stops)

Date: 2026-10-18 (one builder). Follows notes/T6.3-cmp2d.md. On the unit, CP2D's two bars both drew, so reply half-words
8..10 do reach the band pages. The load sat at about 45-52 % before playing, while playing and at the stop, so the
DSP is **not** running out of time. The stop follows LOW MUP from power-on, also with LOW XOVR at 196 Hz; LOW RAT
alone does not cause it, and MID or HIGH MUP don't either. Marks as in the CMP2D note. Nothing flashed, committed, pushed
or posted.

## 1. What CMP2E is

CMP2E = CMP2D's main OS (`CP2D.main.bin`, the same bytes; comp-ui 0.1d already draws half-words 8..11) + the DSP
**CMP2E** (`tools/dspsplice/src/cmp2e/`): CMP2D's sources with breadcrumbs, a heartbeat, a counter, a flag and one
probe switch. Audio: CMP2's sample for sample except in the probe mode (section 4).

| bar | half-word | shows |
|---|---|---|
| LOW, left | 8 | **heartbeat**: 10 px and 20 px, alternating every 750 blocks (0.5 s), counted in `cmp_load_end` |
| MID, left | 9 | **breadcrumb**: the stage id × 3 px (table below) |
| HIGH, left | 10 | guard trips + quiet re-inits since boot, 4 px each, at most 44 px; **50 px** once the master overdrive state (`0x257d08` 16 words, `0x257d68` 8, `0x257d88` 16) has held a NaN or inf (latched: "core alive, audio dead") |
| right, every page | 11 | the highest load since boot, as on CP2D (direct and via the stock getter) |

Breadcrumbs (`cmp_crumb.h`): `CRUMB(id)` = one 32-bit volatile store of (heartbeat | id·1965 << 16) to +0x10 of each
reply page, at the entry of each stage, no call. Ids: 1 glue entry (cmp_load_begin), 2 the magic test / re-init, 3 tail
fetch, 4 tail apply or the fallback knobs, 5 mbcomp_process's control block (mb_control, mb_targets), 6 crossover split,
7/8/9 detector band 0/1/2 (7 also the key detector), 10/11/12 gain computer band 0/1/2, 13 output stage, 14 guard /
restart, 15 the glue's meters, 16 glue exit (our code has finished its block). 16 × 3 = 48 px.

## 3. The band loops and the makeup path in selcc's code (CMP2, the unit's image; sw addresses)

- **Shared, not unrolled** [verified]: a profile of one CMP2 block (three active bands) hits 152 PCs of `mb_run` 96 times
  (3 bands × 32 samples): the detector and gain loops are one body for the three bands. In CMP2E the crumbs inside
  them come from one site each (detector `0x18646f`, key detector `0x18630c`, gain `0x186724`).
- CMP2 ranges: `mb_targets` `0x184697-0x1849c4`, `mbcomp_set` `0x1849f9`, `mb_control` `0x1854ab-0x185ba0`,
  `mb_run` `0x185ec3-0x18726b`, `mbcomp_process` `0x18726b-0x1873b4`; `cmp_tail_apply` `0x18b26d-0x18b631` is
  straight-line, one block per knob: **LOW MUP `0x18b353-0x18b389`**, encoding for encoding the same as MID MUP
  (`0x18b4a0`) and HIGH MUP (`0x18b5ed`) but for the address and the knob index [verified]. So the only band-0-specific
  thing on the makeup path is data (knob 3, band 0's state), not code.

## 4. The probe switch (cmp_tail.c, CMP2E only)

While the held LOW XOVR tail word (word 7, k 75) is ≥ `0x7e00` (knob 126 or 127), LOW MUP (word 6) is still received,
copied and checked by `cmp_tail_fetch` but **not applied**: band 0 keeps its makeup. [verified (p): XOVR glides 68 → 127
while MUP turns, then back to 68; CMP2E equals CMP2 fed the same tails with word 6 held, 38,400 of 38,400 samples;
band 0's MUP knob stays at 20.32 while the switch is on and follows to 127 after.] On the unit: LOW XOVR to max, then
LOW MUP. Still stops → the bytes in transit (a). No stop → our use of the value (b).

## 5. Does a stopped core still show the last crumb?

The SPI2 TX descriptors chain in a ring (`0x2c2998`.next = `0x2c29b4`, `0x2c29b4`.next = `0x2c2998`; buffers `0x2c59d0`,
`0x2c49d0`) [verified, static], so the DMA keeps sending both pages without the core. CP2D's bars still showed after the
stop (owner's photos), and dnfw saw the same with their markers [their measurement]. So a frozen core shows its last
crumb and a frozen heartbeat [inferred, well supported]. One limit: a stop between the two page stores of one crumb
leaves the pages one stage apart, so the MID bar may flicker between two heights: the higher one is the newer one.
While running, the MID bar mostly shows 48 px (stage 16: our code is a small part of the block) with flickers lower
[inferred].
