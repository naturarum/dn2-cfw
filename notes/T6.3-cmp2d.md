# T6.3: CMP2D, a diagnostic build of CMP2 (DSP load meter; the meter path)

Date: 2026-10-18 (one builder). Firmware: Digitone II OS 1.12. Why: CMP2 (`d8551470...`) stops the DSP on the unit when a
band's MUP passes about noon, and the band GR bars never moved on CMP1 or CMP2 (notes/T6.3-cmp2.md; ROADMAP T6.3c).
Marks: **[verified]** = run in sharcvm, the emulator or the build, or read in the bytes; **[inferred]** = reasoned, not
run. Nothing flashed, committed, pushed or posted.

**No mechanism was found that drops half-words 8..10 and delivers 11; the write location is the right one as far as the
image and the simulator show.** What is left is [inferred] and is what CMP2D tests on the unit: (1) the values never reached

## 2. The load measurement (src/cmp2d/cmp_load.c, cmp_clk.s) [verified in sharcvm]

No new hook. At glue entry `cmp_load_begin` reads EMUCLK and walks the frame chain (cmp_fp's frame, cmp_load_begin,
cmp_process, the stub, the master `0x1c1f1e`, the render, the handler; each frame's first word is the caller's I6):
- **handler-entry stamp** = the render's saved R14 (`DM(I6-61)`, `0x1c274f`): the handler reads EMUCLK at entry
  (`0x1c9d89`) and keeps EMUCLK − 1 in R14 [verified: equal to R14 at `0x1c9d8c` every block];
- **reply page** = the handler's `DM(I6-2)`.
- **period** = this stamp − the previous one; **busy** = the stock count of the previous block (entry to `0x1c9e47`), read
  from word 0 of the previous block's page (halves swapped back); **comp** = EMUCLK at glue exit − at glue entry.
- load = busy/period, share = comp/period, for the previous block. The walk is checked (frames rising, steps < 4 KB, the
  page one of the two, the stamp < 2^21 cycles old); a failed check reads nothing and counts in `bad`.
- Kept (CmpLoad, FILL `0x2e2800`, 96 B): peak-hold = max of the current and the previous 1,500-block window (1–2 s);
  the highest load and the highest share since boot, from the 1,501st measured block on (boot transients out).
- Cost: +200 instructions per block over CMP2 (0.04 % est.); stack 1,692 B (CMP2 1,720).

## 3. The readout

Q15 of a fraction of the block period, q = x·32767, h = q/655 px: **1 px = 2 %, a full 50 px bar = 100 %** (it reaches
the bottom screen row). Left bar (x 18-19) of each band page = its own half-word; right bar (x 21-22) = half-word 11.

| half-word | where | value |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | LOW page, left bar | load peak-hold (last 1–2 s) |
| 9 | MID page, left bar | highest load since boot |
| 10 | HIGH page, left bar | highest compressor share since boot |
| 11 | right bar, every band page | highest load since boot again (≤ 32750): direct, and through the stock getter (`0x257c1c` = 20·log10(1 − q/32768) dB; the getter returns q exactly in sharcvm) |

All bars at 1 px = the frame walk never passed. Reading on the unit: left and right bars both move → the direct path
works and CMP1/CMP2's band values were the problem; right only → direct stores to +0x10..+0x15 do not reach the band pages
(then probe +0x04..+0x13, dnfw's proven words); neither → the ColdFire side. After the DSP stops, the bars freeze at
their last values (the DMA keeps sending the pages: dnfw's observation; inferred here): a max load near 100 % points at
running out of time, well below it at a hang or trap.

## 6. On the unit (2026-10-18, the owner's photos) and what followed

Both bars drew on every band page: reply half-words 8..10 reach the band pages, so CMP1/CMP2's band bars were most likely
just under 655 or their bands not compressing [inferred]. The load bars ended about at the box's bottom edge (≈ 45-52 %)
before playing, while playing and at the stop; the compressor's share ≈ 15 %. The DSP is not out of time. The stop
follows LOW MUP. Next: CMP2E, breadcrumbs and a probe switch (notes/T6.3-cmp2e.md).
