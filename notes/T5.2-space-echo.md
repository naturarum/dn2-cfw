# T5.2: a Roland Space Echo (RE-201) style tape echo for the delay slot

Date: 2026-10-12. Code: `fx/spaceecho.c/.h`, tests `fx/test_spaceecho.c` (part of `make test`). Renders, the A/B
page and the SHARC build: `out/T3.11/` (the T3.11 workbench). Mac workbench and SHARC interpreter only: nothing
was spliced into the DSP image, flashed, committed or posted.
Marks: **[verified]** = measured or tested here; **[sourced]** = from a cited document; **[inferred]** = reasoned
or remembered, not checked; **[guess]** = a choice to tune by ear.

## Summary

- **A new delay-slot effect in C, `fx/spaceecho.c`**, on the owner's 8-control design of 2026-10-12: TIME, MODE,
  INTENSITY, AGE, CENTER, BW, WIDTH, PAN. Three playback heads on one tape at 1x, 2x and 3x the head-1 time
  (Roland's own 1:2:3 [sourced]); MODE picks the heads in the RE-202's order; the feedback is the sum of the heads
  that sound; one knob, AGE, for all the wear; a resonant band filter in the loop (CENTER, BW) that is bit-exactly
  off at BW 127; three PAN modes. It reuses the one-head tape delay's proven parts (`fx/tapedelay.c`, FTD1 on the
  unit), which is **unchanged**, and its 155 tests still pass, its renders bit-identical.
- **`make test`: 282 checks, all pass** (127 new). Among them: echoes exactly at the selected heads for all 7
  modes; at INTENSITY 0, BW 127, AGE 0 the echo is the input through the tape saturator **bit for bit** (and
  91.8 dB under the input at -40 dBFS); a 120 s all-knob fuzz then 300 s of silence with no NaN, Inf or denormal
  anywhere, the tape included; every one of the 8 controls jumped on a tone without a click. [verified]
- **SHARC build** (selcc, run in the T3.6 interpreter, 2 s, against the Mac build): **bit-identical** for one clean
  head and for TIME glides and random pan; -131 to -135 dB re peak with AGE and the band filter; -108 dB in a
  worst case with a self-oscillating three-head loop and knob moves. [verified]
- **Cost (selcc, instructions per 32-sample block):** one clean head **18.4k**, one head with AGE, band and ALT
  27.7k, two heads 32.9k, three heads **38.0k**, worst case 48.6k mean (67.9k in the busiest block). FTD1 is 21.5k,
  the stock delay 5.4k. **Each extra head costs about 5.1k** (mostly selcc's code for the Hermite read of two
  tracks). Hand assembly of the head loop would bring all three heads near the stock delay (section 6).
- **State 4,163,232 bytes**: fits the stock delay's DDR area (4,194,908 bytes) with 31.6 KB to spare. Code about
  38 KB (FTD1: 24 KB), which fits in L1 block 2 (section 6.1).
- **A fifth selcc bug**: its preprocessor substitutes macro parameters inside number literals (`1.0f` with a
  parameter named `f`). Worked around; repro in section 6.2.
- **Listen:** `make listen` in `fx/` (after `make stock`), http://127.0.0.1:8711/ab.html: the first seven groups
  are the Space Echo, each with the dry input, the stock delay (emulated), **FTD1** (one head, as on the unit) and
  the new renders. What to play first: section 8.
- **Open for the owner** (section 10): the output level of several heads (three heads singing are about 10 dB
  louder than one), the AGE amounts, the BW range and resonance, MONO's WIDTH meaning, and which control goes
  where on the page (section 9).

## 3. Signal flow

The tape has two tracks, A and B (left and right in MONO mode). Per sample:

```
heads:  for each active head k (1..3): Hermite read of A and B at delay tau_k + wow/flutter offset
        -> output:   L += oLA_k a + oLB_k b,  R += oRA_k a + oRB_k b     (its stereo place: PAN, WIDTH)
        -> feedback: sumA += g_k a,  sumB += g_k b                        (g_k: MODE gain, 0 or 1)
        + AGE hiss and soft crackle (they follow the playback level), on the output and the feedback
        -> wet out (L, R)
record: A = iAL inL + iAR inR + INT x DCblock(fAA sumA + fAB sumB)        (input and feedback matrices: PAN)
        B = iBL inL + iBR inR + INT x DCblock(fBA sumA + fBB sumB)
        -> band filter: resonant 2-pole HP, then 2-pole LP (CENTER, BW), faded out at BW 119..127, off at 127
        -> AGE top-end loss: one-pole low-pass (off at AGE 0)
        -> drive (x resonance trim) into the cubic soft saturator, x 1/drive    -> tape
```

All 23 mixing coefficients (input 4, feedback 4, head gains 3, head outputs 12) glide (20 ms) to the values MODE,
PAN and WIDTH ask for, ramped per sample, so none of those three knobs can click. The output is the wet signal
only; the FX bus adds VOL and the REV send, as for the stock delay.

**PAN, precisely.**
- **MONO (0):** input L on track A, R on track B; feedback on the same track; every head plays A and B through the
  width matrix (L = (1+w)/2 A + (1-w)/2 B, R the mirror). The heads are not panned.
- **ALT (1):** the input, summed to mono, records on track A only; the feedback crosses tracks (A from the heads'
  B, B from their A), so a sound's first echo is on A, its second on B, and so on. Heads 1 and 3 play A at
  position -w (left) and B at +w; head 2 plays A at +w and B at -w. So the **heads alternate sides** (1 left, 2
  right, 3 left at full WIDTH) **and every repeat crosses sides**. One head at full WIDTH is the classic ping-pong
  (tested: L, R, L, each repeat x the INTENSITY gain).
- **RND (2):** the input to mono on both tracks; feedback on the same track; head k plays (A+B)/2 at w x p_k. Each
  p_k jumps to a new uniform random place in -1..+1 once per the head's own period (its current delay: head 1
  every D, head 2 every 2D, head 3 every 3D) and moves there at most 1/24 per control block (a full swing in
  32 ms), the gains ramped per sample: no click (tested 1.01 on the click detector; jump counts exact).

**Wow and flutter, as a tape does it.** FTD1 moves the delay with a fixed pitch deviation. Here the wow/flutter
sources (tapedelay.c's rates: 0.55 Hz wow, 6.5 and 11.3 Hz flutter, plus smoothed noise) are a tape speed
deviation e(t). Its integral M (in samples, leaky with a 5 s time constant so it stays bounded) is kept per
control block in a 16,384-entry history, and head k's delay is offset by M(t - tau_k) - M(t). So head k plays at
pitch 1 + e(t) - e(t - tau_k): the speed now against the speed when that bit of tape was recorded. Slow wow
hardly bends a short head (the tape had nearly the same speed then), the farther heads differ, and every repeat
re-records the wobble. Measured largest pitch deviation per head (heads 1/2/3): AGE 64, head 1 at 1/16: 0.25 /
0.32 / 0.36 %; AGE 64, head 1 at 1/4: 0.39 / 0.44 / 0.31 %; AGE 127: 1.0..1.7 %. [verified] Cost: about 30
operations per control block.

**Transparency.** At BW 127 the band filter is out of the path (the samples never touch it; CENTER then changes
nothing, tested bit-identical with feedback on); at AGE 0 there is no wow and no top-end loss; at INTENSITY 0 the
feedback path is skipped. What remains is the tape saturator (the cubic of FTD1, drive 1 at AGE 0), which holds the
loop and is never bypassed: the test checks the echo against sat(input) bit for bit. At -40 dBFS that is 91.8 dB
under the input. A 15 Hz one-pole DC blocker sits in the feedback path only (not the first echo): without it a
loop above 100 % can latch onto DC.

**Where the differences come from** [verified by switching features one at a time]: a TIME glide alone and RND
alone are bit-identical; AGE 127 alone is -130 dB (the crackle and hiss code's divisions and conversions); a
CENTER move on a resonant band is -117 dB (selcc's division in `fx_svf_g`/`fx_svf_coef` is not always correctly
rounded, as T3.8/T3.11 found); in the worst case a self-oscillating three-head loop grows those differences to
-108 dB. Within the "about -100 dB" the task allows, with that reason.

**Where the time goes** (three heads, `--annotate`): the sample loop 34.7k, the control update 3.1k. In the loop:
each head about 5.1k (the Hermite read of two tracks about 3.4k, the read position 1k, its output and feedback
0.7k), the band filter 4.9k (four state-variable filters), hiss 1.9k, saturator 1.7k, the input/feedback
matrices and the DC blocker about 4k.

**Two C changes that cut it** (both keep the tests and the Mac/SHARC match): the Hermite cubic written as four
weights computed once per head and applied to both tracks (29 instead of 38 float operations per head per sample;
exactly 0, 1, 0, 0 at an integer delay), and each head's read position kept as an index plus a fraction, moved by
the delay's change per sample and set exactly from the delay at every control block (no float/int conversions
per sample). With the gain and wow ramps skipped when still: three heads 43.0k -> 38.0k, one clean head 20.0k ->
18.4k.

**Against FTD1:** one head with everything on costs 27.7k against FTD1's 21.5k (the band filter, the matrices and
the wow history); one clean head 18.4k is cheaper than FTD1 (the filter is off at BW 127); each extra head adds
5.1k.

**Hand assembly** (T3.11 11.4's estimate for one stereo Hermite head: 6,000 -> 900 instructions): three heads about
2.7k, the record path about 1.5k, the rest about 3k: **about 7k per block for the full three-head echo**, near the
stock delay's 5.4k [inferred].

**Budget:** FTD1 brought the handler to 339k per block (+18.7k over stock, T3.8 section 9). The Space Echo in its
place: about +17k (three heads) to +27k (the worst case's mean) more, so roughly 356k-366k of the 666,667 cycles a
1 GHz block has, before DDR stalls (the tape is in DDR; three heads read 24 floats per sample instead of 8)
[inferred; instructions are not cycles].

### 6.1 Memory

- **State** `sizeof(SpaceEcho)` = 4,163,232 bytes: the tape 4,096,000 (512,000 frames x 2 tracks: 10.67 s), the
  wow history 65,536, the rest about 1.7 KB. With a 44-byte glue header like FTD1's `TdSlot`: 4,163,276 of the
  stock delay's 4,194,908 bytes (31.6 KB spare). Longest head-1 time 3.54 s (head 3: 10.6 s).
- **Code** about 38 KB of selcc output (8,815 instruction lines against tapedelay's 5,592 = 24,464 bytes,
  T3.8). FTD1's code sits at `0x310000-0x315f90` in L1 block 2; 38 KB from `0x310000` ends at about `0x319800`,
  below the PM cache at `0x31c000` but not below `0x318000` if that cache were 32 KB (T3.8 section 3); starting at
  `0x308400` (right after the flanger's state) it ends near `0x311c00` either way [inferred: estimated size, not
  linked].

### 6.2 A fifth selcc bug [verified]

selcc's preprocessor substitutes a macro parameter inside a number literal. Repro:

```c
#define M(f) (1.0f + (f))
#define N(e) (1e-6f * (e))
float g(float x, float y) { return M(x) + N(y); }
```

`selcc -E` gives `(1.0x + (x)) + (1y-6f * (y))`; clang gives `1.0f` and `1e-6f`. Here it stopped the build
(`expected RParen`), but with a numeric argument it could compile silently to another constant (`2e3` in a macro
with a parameter `e`, called with `5`, becomes `253`). The Space Echo's read macro had a parameter named `f`; it is
now `fq`. The other effects' macros were checked: no parameter name appears as a literal's suffix or exponent
letter in a macro body. Worth reporting upstream with the four of T3.11 (11.3, 12.7).

## 7. The selcc rules followed

As tapedelay.c (T3.11 section 11, 12.7): state copied into locals for the sample loop and written back; no calls in
the sample loop (statement macros: `SE_READ`, `SE_TAPE_GLIDE`, `SE_GLIDE`, fxmath.h's `FX_*`); every per-block flag
set with an `if` statement (bug 1); no `float a = X, b = a` declarations (bug 4); no table indexed by a fresh
integer (bug 3: MODE's heads are an `if` chain); bit casts through fxmath.h's pointer helpers (the union bug);
no shifts by an immediate (`make sharc` counts 0); no macro parameter named like a literal suffix (bug 5).

## 9. The 8 controls on the stock delay page

The stock delay page (T3.11 section 2): A TIME, B X (ping-pong), C WID, D FDBK, E HPF, F LPF, G (no parameter),
H REV; LEVEL = VOL. Six parameter rows (TIME X WID FDBK HPF LPF) plus the bus's REV and VOL. The Space Echo needs
eight values plus REV and VOL.

**The six rows map naturally** (same knob range, same frame words; only labels change on the ColdFire side):

| enc | stock row | Space Echo | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | TIME | **TIME** | same law, same meaning (head 1) |
| B | X | **PAN** | X is a 2-state switch; PAN has 3 states. In the DSP's parameter struct X arrives as 0/1 (+4, T3.8 glue), so RND needs either the raw frame word or another trick [inferred: to check how the render's unpack turns the X word into 0/1] |
| C | WID | **WIDTH** | the stock WID is shown bipolar (-64..+63); the value range is the same 0..127 |
| D | FDBK | **INTENSITY** | same meaning; the frame carries a fractional low byte |
| E | HPF | **CENTER** | |
| F | LPF | **BW** | LPF 127 (stock "open") = BW 127 (no filter): the stock default keeps its meaning |
| G | (none) | **MODE** | a new row: 7-value enumeration |
| H | REV | REV | the bus's send, unchanged |
| LEVEL | VOL | VOL | unchanged |

**Which two need new rows: MODE and AGE.** MODE fits on G (the one encoder without a parameter); AGE has no
encoder left on one page. Options for the main session's study (T5.0: pages are cheap, rows are the cost; T6.0:
8 extra values per send effect can travel in the frame's unused tail words):
1. **Two pages** (recommended if a second delay page is built anyway): page 1 as above with MODE on G; AGE on page
   2, with seven free slots (candidates: input drive, the tape's top loss apart from AGE, a spring-reverb amount
   (the RE-201's other half), a glide time, FREEZE).
2. **One page:** AGE on G, MODE on B (the X row turned into a 7-value enumeration), and PAN folded into WIDTH as a
   bipolar knob: left of centre ALT (more left = wider), centre mono, right of centre RND (more right = wider).
   MONO mode's "input stereo as it comes" is lost there. [guess]
3. **A DSP-only first version** (no ColdFire change, stock labels on screen, like FTD1): TIME, X -> PAN (MONO/ALT),
   WID -> WIDTH, FDBK -> INTENSITY, HPF -> CENTER, LPF -> BW; MODE and AGE fixed in the glue (for example MODE
   1+2+3 and AGE 48, or MODE 1+2). A possible carrier for one of them without a new row: frame word `0xa18` (k 40,
   "Delay FX Routing"), which the ColdFire sends and the stock DSP never reads (T6.0 1.2), if its UI value range
   suits [inferred: not checked what that setting's screen offers].

## 10. Questions for the owner

1. **Several heads are louder.** The heads are summed at equal level (as I believe the RE-201 does [guess]): a
   sustained sound with three heads, or three heads running away (about 3.0 wet against 0.9 for one head, about
   +10 dB), is much louder than one head. On the unit that hits the FX bus ceiling at a high VOL. Keep it, or scale
   the heads' outputs by 1/sqrt(heads) (a one-line change: separate echoes 4.8 dB quieter with three heads, a
   three-head runaway +4.8 dB instead of +10)? The feedback would stay the plain sum either way.
2. **AGE:** is 64 a "vintage machine" and 127 "worn out"? Is the top-end loss (6.5 kHz per pass at 64) too dark
   for the middle of the knob? Wow and flutter now act per head like a real tape (a short head hardly wobbles with
   slow wow): does that sound right next to FTD1?
3. **BW:** half an octave at the narrowest with a +6 dB singing peak, resonance from 8 octaves down. Narrower,
   more resonance, or should resonance start later? Is CENTER 50 Hz..12.8 kHz the right span?
4. **INTENSITY taper** (x^1.5): three heads run away from about 57, two from 75, one from about 120. Is that
   spread right?
5. **PAN:** in MONO, WIDTH is the echoes' own stereo (0 mono, 127 the input's stereo). Should MONO instead be
   strictly mono, with WIDTH doing nothing there? In ALT a hard-panned head is +3 dB on its side (equal power, so
   that WIDTH 0 is the same level in every mode): fine, or prefer the sides at the middle's level?
6. **TIME glide:** the bend now starts and ends smoothly (motor inertia, 20 ms to full speed). Worth giving FTD1
   the same (it has the corner)?
7. **Page:** which layout of section 9 (two pages with AGE on page 2; one page with WIDTH bipolar; or a DSP-only
   first version with MODE and AGE fixed)?
8. **Runaway in the low end:** with three heads at full INTENSITY, no band filter and AGE 0, the loop can settle on
   a low tone (24 Hz at TIME 23). Closing BW a little stops it. Leave it, or put a fixed gentle low cut (about
   40 Hz) in the feedback path?
