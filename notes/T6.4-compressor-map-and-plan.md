# T6.4: the stock master compressor mapped, and the plan for the 3-band compressor

Date: 2026-10-13 (workflow planner, five lenses each checked by a skeptic). Firmware: Digitone II OS 1.12.
Marks: **[verified]** = checked against the bytes, or measured in sharcvm or the digikit emulator (by a lens
and, unless said otherwise, re-run by its skeptic); **[inferred]** = reasoned from structure or another
project's notes, not run. Nothing was built into a candidate, flashed, committed or posted. All runs used
scratch directories under `out/T6.4/` (section 8). Findings a skeptic did not let stand are dropped or
corrected here (section 7).

The owner's decisions this plan follows: a 3-band master compressor; two non-overlapping crossover points;
three fully independent compressors "with the same basic design as the Elektron one", summed back; one
sidechain setting for all bands; one page per band (LOW, MID, HIGH); the crossover knob on LOW (low/mid) and
MID (mid/high), an empty box in that slot on HIGH; makeup gain (MUP) per band; no p-locks; the stock default
dial as placeholder glyph; CPU "towards the limits; cut back only if the result gets unfriendly".

## Summary

- **The stock compressor** (`0xb8541d`, called at sw `0x1c215c`) is a stereo-linked, per-sample peak
  compressor: a first-order side-chain filter, max(|L|,|R|) in dB, a hard knee, "smooth decoupled"
  release-then-attack smoothing in dB, makeup 0..+24.08 dB, a wet clip at ±1.0, then a table-law dry/wet MIX.
  It costs **16.6k instructions per block** (5 % of the stock handler) [verified]. Its gain reduction feeds a
  **live 2-px bar on the stock Compressor page** and the Overbridge status record [verified].
- **One band of our `fx/mbcomp.c` already matches it** to 0.002-0.016 dB on the static curve and ~1 % on
  times [verified]. The gaps: the side-chain filter (stock is 1st order and also filters self-detection;
  ours is 2nd order on the key only), the wet clip, the smoothing of MUP/MIX, and **MIX 0**, which in stock is
  a near pass-through of the raw input (gain 0.99999404) [verified here] but in mbcomp the allpassed band sum
  [read from its code].
- **Pages:** MIXER becomes {LOW, MID, HIGH, INT MIX 1, INT MIX 2, FX MIX, EXT MIX} (7 pages). Each band page:
  A THR, B ATK, C REL, D MUP, E RAT (stock positions), F crossover (empty on HIGH), G SCS, H MIX, LEVEL VOL.
  SCF moves to the FX Mixer page, knob D (empty in stock). Run in the emulator as a hot-patched mock-up
  [verified]. Each band page gets its own GR bar.
- **Rows:** GT01's 17 rows (k 70..86) for the bands and crossovers; the stock rows SCS/SCF/MIX/VOL as the
  globals; the stock THR..RAT rows (k 60..64) are hidden and kept for old kits and the fallback.
- **Transport and storage:** four ColdFire sites, none used by any mod: the frame build (a 30-word tail at
  `0xa80`, magic + sequence + 27 values + check) and the FX record's 28 unused words (+0x60..+0x97).
  Prototyped in emulator RAM and sharcvm: stock code never reads either; stock OS loads our kits unchanged
  [verified].
- **CPU:** the 3-band adds about +25k (typical) to +36k (worst) instructions per block over the stock
  compressor. Estimated load **~73 % typical, ~75-77 % worst with FTD1, ~84 % worst with the Space Echo**
  (stock 64 %, GT01 68 %) [inferred from measured instruction counts]. No cut to the per-sample stock design
  is needed; hand assembly is the reserve.
- **Stages:** CMP0 (DSP only: 3 bands on the stock knobs, fixed crossovers) → CMP1 (the three pages and
  meters, band knobs not yet wired) → CMP2 (frame tail: band knobs live, not saved) → CMP3 (kit storage).
  Each is flashable and testable alone, like FTN1 → GT00 → GT01.
- **Owner questions** (section 6.3): where SCF (or MIX) goes; the crossover ranges; a 1.00 ratio step; how old
  projects open.

## 1. The stock master compressor, fully mapped

### 1.1 Where it runs

| step | address | what | mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| audio task → handler → render | `0x1c9fe7` → `0x1c9d6b` → `0x1c2712` | runs on render (command-3) frames only; command 0/1/2 frames make no write to its state or buffers | verified |
| unpack | `0x1c2efe-0x1c2fc5` | k 60..67 → struct `0x257964`, from the **previous** block's frame image `0x25c48c` (672 words = frame bytes `0x000-0xa7f`, copy loop `0x1c302d`; the tail `0xa80..` is not copied) | verified |
| voices, FX bus | `0x1c8ef1`, `0x1c1738` | flanger/delay/reverb (our FLG1/FTD1 stubs run here) | verified |
| master | `0x1c1f1e` (call `0x1c305d`) | track buses → routing sums → SCS pointer select → **compressor CJUMP at `0x1c215c`** → step master → overdrive → pattern volume | verified |
| GR meter getter | CJUMP `0xb8552b` at `0x1c3108`, store at `0x1c3117` | reply half-word 11 (byte +0x16) | verified |

Before the compressor [verified]: 16 stereo track buses `0x258f68+0x100t` (R at +0x80), built from the voice
rows at ×14.1254 (+23.0 dB). Routing loop `0x1c2038`: bit t of flag word `0x257aa8` (frame `0x2c`, plus
`0x2e`<<16) drops track t (probably the mute [inferred]); otherwise bit t of frame `0xd4` (struct `0x257984`)
sends it to the **comp bus** `0x25b968/0x25b9e8`, else to the **bypass bus** `0x25bb68/0x25bbe8`. IN L
`0x25bc68`, IN R (dual) `0x25bd68` go by frame `0xd6` bits 0/1; the FX returns `0x25b868` by bit 2; all at
×14.1254. An external input at level 127 lands at +3.0 dB re codec on the comp bus.

After the compressor [verified]: `out = ODchain(2·(comp_out + bypass)) × 1.6384 × (VOL/127)² × (1 − OD/254)`,
ramped over 32 samples. Small signal from the compressor output to the handler output: ×3.2768 at OD 0,
VOL 127; a soft knee from 0.19 to 0.24 compressor output, then a flat ceiling at 0.6930 (−3.19 dBFS at the
codec) that sits after pattern volume. At OD 64 the ceiling comes at ~0.08. The stock wet clip at ±1.0 never
acts before this ceiling.

### 1.4 Signal path inside `0xb8541d` [verified: listings `out/T6.4/stockcomp/lst_stages.txt`, sharcvm]

Entry `0xb8541d` builds {inL, inR, scL, scR, outL, outR} and calls `0xb85396`, which runs five stages:

| stage | address | per sample | instr/block |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC filter | `0xb84fd3` (biquad `0xb809f2`) | 1st-order bilinear on scL, scR: y = b0x + b1x1 − a1y1. idx < 64: LP {T, T, 2T−1}; idx ≥ 64: HP {(1−T)/2, −(1−T)/2, T}; analog corner fa = 20 Hz × 1000^(j/63), j = idx mod 64, no prewarp. Digital corners: 20 Hz (0) … 278 Hz (24) … 1.6 kHz (40) … 14.03 kHz (63); HP 20 Hz (64, the neutral setting) … 14.03 kHz (127). Coefficients retargeted every block with a 32-sample linear ramp. Always on, also for SCS 0 | 3,529 |
| detector + gain computer | `0xb85070` | lev = max(\|scL_f\|, \|scR_f\|, 2^−23); dB = 20·log10; gc = 0 if dB ≤ thr, else thr + (dB − thr)/r − dB. Hard knee, peak, no RMS, no lookahead, channels linked | 6,539 |
| smoothing | `0xb85111` | y1 = min(gc, cr·y1 + (1−cr)·gc) (instant attack, release one-pole), saved +0x170; y2 = ca·y2 + (1−ca)·y1, saved +0x16c | 299 |
| gain, makeup, clip | `0xb851e5` | g = 10^((y2 + mk)/20) per sample, mk = 24.0824·(MUP−1) dB with ×16 in the apply; wet = fclip(16·g·x, 1.0) | 5,407 |
| MIX | `0xb852d2` | out = T[m]·wet + T[127−m]·dry, dry = the raw comp input; each gain follows cur = target + 0.995·(cur − target) per sample (τ 4.16 ms) | 787 |
| total | | | **16,609-16,642** (of 320,331-320,394 for the whole stock handler) |

The stage split is the lens's measurement (`stage_cost.txt`); the total was re-run by the skeptic.

Measured behaviour [verified]: 63 % attack time = the ATK table τ (0.10/2.58/10.0/25.4/61.9/99.9 ms at
ATK 0/20/40/64/100/127); release ≈ τ_rel + τ_atk (398 ms at REL 64 + ATK 64). MIX table: T[127] = 1.0,
T[64] = −6.0 dB, T[63] = −6.56, T[32] = −24, T[1] = −92.25, T[0] = 0 (linear zero). T[m] + T[127−m] is
−0.26 dB at 63/64 and −2.3 dB near 32/48/96.

**MIX 0 is a near pass-through** [verified here, `out/T6.4/plan/mix0.py`, `mix0b.py`]: at MIX 0 the wet gain
settles at exactly 0 and the dry gain at 0.99999404 (the float fixed point of the 0.995 one-pole on T[127] =
1.0), at any THR/RAT/MUP. The comp output is the raw comp input × 0.99999404 (max |out − in| 4.1e-6 on a
0.7 peak). MIX 0 is the stock default, so every kit that does not use the compressor relies on this.

**The GR bar** [verified]: the getter `0xb8552b` reads only y2 (`0x257c1c`, dB, makeup not included) and
returns 1 − clamp(10^(y2/20), 0, 1); `fix_by 15` and a 16-bit store at `0x1c3117` put it at TX reply byte
+0x16. Over SPI2 it lands in the ColdFire raw RX buffer `0x80001a20` (`FUN_400cf922` first copies the previous
reply to `0x800053a4`). Two readers:
- the screen: `FUN_400cfbf4` (`move.w (0x80001a36).l,D0; rts`, one caller) → h = q/655 px →
  `FUN_401147f0(bmp, 0x12, 0x32, 0x13, 0x33 − h, 1)`: a 2-px bar at x 18-19 hanging down from the top of the
  box (y-up coordinates; screen row 13 down), 0..50 px. The box itself is 24 px tall, so it holds about 6 dB;
  beyond that the bar runs below it. Linear in 1 − g: 1 dB = 5 px, 3 dB = 14, 6 dB = 24, 10 dB = 34,
  20 dB = 45. Redrawn every UI frame (~30 Hz).
- Overbridge: the audio ISR `FUN_40025e36` copies `0x800053ba` → `0x4058f82a` (status record `0x4058f7f4`
  +0x36), sent by `FUN_40003118` only in USB mode 6 (vendor class, PID 0x0b34; "Overbridge" by name
  [inferred]). dnfw saw +0x16 change on the unit on 1.11.

Option (b) (keep page 20 as a global page, 8 pages) also ran; it leaves 12 empty knob boxes across four

**Page ids 40-42, not 37-39** [planner decision]: 37 is our unflashed fx-page2 example's id
(`vendor/elekloader/examples/fx-page2-dn2`, built into `out/T5.0/build/flanger-FP01.syx`), and T5.0 plans

fx-page2's `pagerec.s` do) [inferred design]. Ids 40-42 themselves are untested (37-39 were); CMP1's

### 2.3 Meters, sidechain, globals

- **One sidechain for all bands**: SCS (k 65) and SCF (k 66). The stock master already puts the chosen bus

  itself; otherwise every band detects the shared key.
- **Same as stock per band**: SCF filters each detector input, as stock always does (neutral 64 = HP 20 Hz);
  for self-detection each band's own signal is filtered, for an external key the key is filtered once.
- **Band meters**: the DSP writes Q15 (1 − g_band) into reply half-words 8, 9, 10 (bytes +0x10/+0x12/+0x14,

  stock place with the stock law. Half-word 11 keeps the stock meaning, fed with the **deepest** band's y2

  (`dsp-reply:0x10-0x15`). Half-words 2-9 and the tail are used by dnfw's Waverider on 1.11 (soft overlap,
  section 6.1).
- **Globals' home**: MIX on every band page (H); VOL on LEVEL; SCF on FX Mixer D. The routing words

## 3. Storage and the frame protocol

### 3.1 Where the values live [verified unless marked]

- The FX record `fxSetupStorage_v0_t` (0xa0 B) sits at stored kit +0x16ac. +0 version (must be 0);
  +4 + 2·id for ids 0..45 through the maps `0x401fd160` (k → id) and `0x401fd278` (id → k); **+0x60..+0x97:
  28 words the serializer `FUN_400dcf34` never writes and the loader `FUN_400dcef0` never reads**; +0x98
  routing; +0x9c zero. In 2,176 real kits these bytes (kit +5900..5956) are zero (DNX's count, spot-checked
  in their docs [inferred]).
- A kit save keeps exactly k 25..69; k 70..100 are neither saved nor reset by a load (T6.5 §5). Today
  (GT01) the band rows carry over from kit to kit and are lost at power-off.
- Single knob edits reach the working image through FxSetup vtable slot 17 (`0x401da4f0` = `0x4002ffca`,
  updateMirror). For k ≥ 70 the map gives id 0, so stock writes the value into the id-0 slot (stored +4)
  ("the fold sink"); a reload copies it into live FX k 0. The next full serialize puts live k 24 back
  there; FX k 0 is not framed, so this is harmless [inferred].

### 3.3 Record encoding (CMP3)

| stored kit | FX record | content |
|---|---|---|
| +0x170c + 2(k−70), k 70..96 | +0x60..+0x95 | the live word (value << 8), raw |
| +0x1742 | +0x96 | check word `0xC3B1` (layout 1) |

- **Raw values, not XOR with defaults** [planner change to the prototype]: the prototype stored live XOR Z[k]
  so that zeros decode to Z. With a check word the XOR adds nothing, and it would force Z to be frozen
  forever (changing a Z would silently change every saved kit).
- **Load**: check word present → each word range-checked against its row (e.g. RAT ≤ 0x800), out of range →
  the row default. **Check word missing** (every kit saved so far, a kit saved by a stock OS, an export) →
  **seed**: all three bands get the kit's stock k 60..64 (THR, ATK, REL, MUP; RAT index + 1),
  Low/Mid = 0 (30 Hz), Mid/High = 127 (16 kHz). The MID band then covers 30 Hz-16 kHz: the kit opens as a
  full-band compressor with its old settings, close to the old sound [inferred; measured in CMP3]. The hook
  runs after the stock loader, so live k 60..64 are already loaded. A record with version ≠ 0 makes
  `FUN_400dddb6` call `FUN_400e7140` (row defaults for k 0..100) and the hook skips the decode [verified].
- **Check word as a constant**, not a checksum: the UI task's slot-17 wrapper and the storage job's
  serializer can interleave; with a constant any mix is still a record of real values.

Stock compatibility [verified on the stock code in the emulator, GT01/GT00/FTN1 warm snapshots]:

| case | result |
|---|---|
| stock OS loads a kit saved by us | live kit byte-identical to loading it without our words |
| stock OS re-saves in place | the 0x2a00 record byte-identical, our words kept |
| stock +Drive kit save (`FUN_4003ff02`, BSS buffer `0x405cb3dc`), exports (`FUN_400e4942`, `FUN_400e4a52`, MmcFs::vslot29) | our words zeroed → seeded on our firmware |
| `FUN_400d5b38` kit branch (uncleared stack buffer; SysEx/Transfer by guess [inferred]) | garbage; rejected by the check word except ~2^−16 |
| v11 import `FUN_40012a60` | leaves our words as they were in the destination buffer (low risk [inferred]) |

Prototype [verified, `out/T6.4/store-frame/store/`]: 464 B in emulator RAM; the hooked serializer differs from
stock only at stored kit +0x170c..+0x1743; the hooked loader restores k 70..96 exactly; on GT1T, pattern copy
and paste, FX page copy/clear/paste: 127 of 129 working-image records identical to T6.5's runs, the other two
differ only at our words and decode to the live values.

### 3.4 The frame tail (CMP2)

| tail word | frame byte | DSP rx byte | content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `0xa80` | rx + 0xa80 | magic `0xC3B1` (low byte = layout; a v2 tail changes it) |
| 1 | `0xa82` | | sequence, +1 per frame |
| 2..28 | `0xa84..0xab8` | | the smoothed row-16 words of k 70..96 (k = 68 + word) |
| 29 | `0xaba` | | ~sum of words 0..28 (16 bit) |

- **ColdFire**: `cf_tail` writes the data halves of the last 30 PUSHR entries (`0x80003a12 + 4j`; the stock
  driver writes only their 0x8001 command halves), and only when DSPI2 SR bit 28 (EOQF) says the previous
  transfer is done. 90 B of code, ~209 instructions per frame at 1,500 Hz. 300 handler passes on GT01, GT00
  and FTN1: wire payload, TX buffer and staging identical to stock; tail valid 300/300; sequence +1; RAM
  differs only at the hook, code, counter and tail halves [verified].
- **DSP**: SPI2 receives 0xabc bytes into rx = `0x2c29d0 + 0x1000·DM(0x2c0450)` (`0x1ca020`; flipped by the
  callback `0x1ca04a`), so the tails are at `0x2c3450` and `0x2c4450` [verified; which one is current is
  inferred, and the reader does not depend on it]. No stock, FLG1 or FTD1 instruction reads them; output is
  bit-identical with the tail full or zero over 1,200 randomised blocks and with real wire frames
  [verified twice, independently]. angellinares found the same on 1.11 (issue #49, 2026-10-13).
- **Reader** (called every block from the compressor stub): copy both tails with 32-bit loads, validate the
  copies (magic, ~sum), take the newer of the valid ones by the signed 16-bit difference **between the two
  buffers**, accept it if its sequence ≠ the held one, else keep the last values. Comparing with the held
  sequence only for equality removes the prototype's lock-out (with "newer than held", a 21.8 s pause in calls
  makes new tails look older) [inferred]. Values = word/32512. The selcc prototype costs 2,039
  instructions per call with 32-bit loads (6,881 with 16-bit loads) [verified].
- **Skew**: k 60..67 arrive through the block-old image, the tail live, so they are one block (0.67 ms)
  apart. Ignored [planner decision].
- A kit change, paste, clear or undo reaches row 16 for k 70..96 through the lazy whole-block recopy
  (`FUN_4003f8ce` → `FUN_40025b6e` → `FUN_40025444`, 0xca bytes, inside the sequencer-event loop) [verified
  static path]. The emulator never reaches that loop (stopped sequencer, T3.3), so it is a unit check.
- The tail is now full (2 + 27 + 1 words). Send-FX second pages (24 values) need the kit padding
  +0x28af..+0x29ff and a multiplexed tail v2.

## 4. The DSP side

### 4.1 The splice

- **Process**: retarget the CALL at `0x1c215c` (core `0x3842b8`) to `fxh_comp`, a tools/dspsplice stub
  (`stub.py`, steps `[("c", "cmp_process")]`, R8 = params, R12 = io) whose save set (R0-R15, I0-I5,
  M1-M4, MODE1) covers I2, I3, I5 and R15. It returns with SIMD off. The save area `0x2e6800` is shared
  with the FX stubs, which have returned by then [inferred].
- **Init**: retarget `0x1c16bb` (core `0x382d76`) to `fxh_comp_init` = `cmp_init()`, then a tail jump to the
  stock `0xb85501`, so the stock state, defaults and `0x257e48` are still set up (the FLG1 pattern).
- **Meter**: `0xb8552b` at `0x1c3108` stays; the glue writes the deepest band's y2 (dB ≤ 0) to
  `0x257c1c` every block. Without that write the stock bar and the Overbridge GR read 0 [verified].
- **Glue I/O**: io→n = 32; io+4 → {comp L, comp R, sc L, sc R} (fxio.h needs this 4-input variant);
  io+0xc → {`0x25ba68`, `0x25bae8`}. Read the pointers every call (the master rebuilds the array each block).
  Write the fully mixed result into out; the master then adds the bypass bus and doubles.
- **Fallback when no valid tail has ever arrived** (a main OS without comp-link): CMP0/CMP1 run the bands from
  struct `0x257964`; from CMP2 on, call the stock `0xb8541d` instead, so the DSP image is bit-exact stock on
  such a main OS [planner decision].

### 4.4 CPU budget

Calibration [inferred]: the stock handler is 320,331 instructions per block in sharcvm and dnfw measured stock
at 63-65 % of 666,667 cycles (1 GHz, 1,500 frames/s) on the unit, so load ≈ (1.30 × instructions + 11k) /
666,667. The 3-band costs v2's measured count [verified] plus ~5.3k for items 1, 6, 9, 10, the tail reader
(2.0k) and glue (0.2k) [inferred], minus the stock compressor's 16.6k.

| build | instructions per block | est. load |
|---|---|---|
| stock 1.12 | 320.3k | 64 % (unit: 63-65 %) |
| GT01 = FLG1 + FTD1, mean / max | 339.0k / 342.5k | 68 % / 68 % |
| + 3-band, typical (v2 defaults 36.3k) | 364k | ~73 % |
| + 3-band, worst (v2 W4 46.9k max, every control moving) on FTD1's max / fuzz-worst block | 378k / 386k | ~75 % / ~77 % |
| Space Echo 3 heads (mean) + 3-band typical | 381k | ~76 % |
| Space Echo worst block (67.9k) + 3-band worst | 421k | ~84 % |
| reserve: crossover + gain loops by hand (−27k) | | −5 points |

The owner's planned reverb is not in these sums. Instruction counts are not cycles: selcc's load/store-heavy
code may stall differently, and whether the audio task survives an occasional overrun is unknown. A real
number needs the idle-time meter (G6/T4.1, dnfw's method).

Optimisations, in order:
1. v2's structure (done in scratch): −15k worst vs the committed C [verified].
2. Stock tables read in place (item 3): no table lookups of our own, −1 KB.
3. At MIX 0 skip exp2, apply and the wet sum while the wet gain is exactly 0 (bit-exact, since 0·wet = 0
   for finite wet); keep the split and detector for the meter: about −5..−9k on every kit that leaves the
   compressor dry [inferred].
4. Idle bands skip detection (already in the committed C).
5. Smaller: log2-unit arithmetic (−0.4k), degree-3 polynomials (−1.5k, 0.001 dB) [inferred].
6. Reserve, only if the unit is unfriendly: hand assembly. The crossover and gain loops alone bring the
   3-band to ~13.9k at the defaults and ~18.1k at the worst (still above stock); below stock (6-9k) also
   needs the key/level path (5.4k) and the output loop (4.2k) [inferred from measured profiles].

Stack: FTD1's deepest point was 1,516 B of the 4,000 B audio task stack; the master call sits deeper than the
FX bus, so CMP0 measures the watermark.

## 6. Risks and open questions

### 6.1 Risks

1. **A crash in our master code is audible at boot**: the compressor runs on every render frame whatever
   its settings. dnfw saw a stopped core loop its last two output pages (a 750 Hz tone) until a power cycle
   [inferred from their hardware runs]. Recovery path: T1.5.
2. **NaN/inf latches the master forever** [verified], and SHARC NaN compares differ from the Mac's, so Mac
   A/B renders cannot show how the DSP fails [verified]. Item 10 of 4.3 is required, not optional.
3. **CPU**: instruction counts calibrated on stock code; ~84 % at the worst with the Space Echo, reverb not
   counted; no load meter on the unit yet.
4. **L1 block 2 contention** with the Space Echo (code range depends on the delay slot) and the PM-cache
   bound `0x318000`.
5. **Old projects change sound slightly** even when seeded (three detectors, crossovers at 30 Hz/16 kHz); MIX 0
   kits stay bit-exact by design (CMP0 proof c).
6. **Level**: three bands × up to +24 dB makeup into ×3.28 and the 0.693 ceiling: hard clipping is easy, as
   with stock at high MUP.
7. **Stock saves zero our words** (+Drive kit save, exports): such kits come back seeded, not as they were.
8. **Forward compatibility**: +0x60..+0x97 are the unused id slots 46..73; a future Elektron OS that adds FX
   parameters would likely use them (1.11's and 1.12's maps both have 46 ids) [inferred].
9. **UI**: ids 40-42 and the DUAL insert change are untested; the band rows' 0x400+ ids with the Overbridge
   plugin; the FX Mixer page changes (SCF on D).
10. **Frame path after paste/clear/kit change** runs only with the sequencer, so only the unit shows it.
11. **Stock tables read in place** tie the DSP mod to the 1.12 image.
12. **Reply bytes**: half-words 8/9/10 overlap dnfw's 1.11 Waverider word 4 (+0x10); no conflict on our 1.12
    builds (nothing of ours touches the reply), a soft overlap for community work.

## 7. What the skeptics changed (lens reports corrected or dropped)

- The stock Compressor page **does** draw a GR bar from half-word 11 (`0x80001a36`); "the screen shows no
  GR meter" was wrong. The bar's y axis points up in firmware coordinates; it holds ~6 dB inside the box.
- SE01 uses `0x308400-0x311c3e`, not all of `0x308400-0x318000`; `0x311c40-0x318000` stays free. The block-1
  tail after FLG1 starts at `0x2e1c38`, not `0x2e1938`. L2 is not needed.
- mbcomp's SCF is not "fitted to stock": stock is first order with symmetric table corners.
- The worst block is W4 (all controls moving): 46.9k for v2, 61.7k for the committed C, not W3.
- Hand assembly of two loops does not bring the compressor below stock at the worst (~18.1k).
- k ≥ 70 read 0 only in new kits (row defaults); a loaded kit keeps the previous values.
- Option (b) has 12 empty boxes, not 7.
- The storage prototype's row defaults (0) and its Z placeholders disagreed for 14 of 17 rows; this plan drops
  the XOR and sets real row defaults.
- dnfw: fxmod is still on their site (the song-data withdrawal was LFO4's, now restored); our T6.5 §8 and
  memory already say dnfw grew the table first. Reply word 2 is their block counter, not idle_load.
- Smaller: release times match within 0.3 % only for REL 30..90 (1.4 % at REL 0); render 2 of the
  program-material test was 8:1 with makeup knob 24 (+4.55 dB), not 6:1; mbcomp's HP corner is within 4.8 %
  up to v 112; the ColdFire has 8 more dead readers of the reply head (`0x400cfb78..0x400cfbd8`,
  `0x400cfc58`); "0 of 6,000 random tails" is not a measured false-accept rate.
- Earlier notes this corrects (not edited here): T3.4's ~19k → 16.6k measured; T3.11's MIX sum "0 dB at
  64" → −0.26 dB; T5.0's "formatter is a case label" → a function pointer.
