## Summary

CMP0 = GT01 (on the unit) with Elektron's master compressor `0xb8541d` replaced by the 3-band compressor
`fx/mbcomp.c`. The three bands sit on the stock Compressor page's knobs: THR, ATK, REL, MUP and RAT drive all three
bands with one value; SCS, MIX and VOL are stock; SCF is dropped (the detectors run at the neutral 64); the
crossovers are fixed at 196 Hz and 2.61 kHz. The ColdFire side is GT01's, unchanged.

W3: −60 dB, 20:1, ATK 0, REL 0, MUP 60 ↔ 68 and MIX 60 ↔ 64 every block; W4 adds THR, ATK, REL 0 ↔ 4 (and SCF,
which CMP0 ignores) every block. "Key" is SCS 18 (the IN bus); self-detection is SCS 0, the stock default. The
worst is W5 at 75.8 % (verify.json `d_cost.worst`). The three self-detection rows on noise and the key row with
MIX jumping reproduce the review's `out/T6.3/review-dsp3/t4_cost_crossfade.json` to the instruction (378,610,

Why self-detection is the dearer path [verified, `fx/mbcomp.c` `mb_run` stage 2]: since plan change #1 a key is
filtered once (one detector loop, into `buf_key`, read by all three bands), while self-detection runs the
first-order detector filter over each band (three loops): +4.7k to +5.5k instructions per block on the mean and
+4.9k on the block maximum in the pairs above. T6.4's worst case had picked a key because in v2 the key path carried
the extra filter; CMP0 reversed that, and the first run of (d) kept the key. MIX jumping between 0 and 64 adds
+1.05k on the mean and +1.4k on the block maximum: for 32 blocks after the MIX index reaches or leaves 0 the output
takes the dry-crossfade loop (raw and band sum mixed per sample), the dearest output loop. RAT moving every block
(W5) adds +0.3k.

FTD1 alone: 67.8 % (68.6 % with the noise). The compressor adds about 3.8 points at MIX 0 and 5.5 at MIX 39 (it
replaces the stock compressor's 16.6k). T6.4 4.4 had estimated +25k typical to +36k worst; the built code adds
+19.3k (MIX 0) to +28.3k (MIX 39) on program material, and up to +33.4k in the worst block, because the MIX-0 cheap

## 8. Limits, and decisions for the owner

1. **The DSP load is over both targets by estimate** (section 5): typical by 1.6 to 3.3 points, and the worst case,
   about 75.8 % [inferred calibration], by about 0.8 points over the ~75 % target. The roadmap wants the load meter
   (T4.1/T4.2) first. The owner decides whether CMP0 goes on the unit before the meter. If it does, crackles or
   dropouts are the meter. Levers: hand-written crossover and gain loops (about −27k, −5 points [inferred]), then
   the per-band detector loops and the output loop (T6.4 4.4 reserve); per-slot hot swap (roadmap). The DSP code
   was not changed for this: nothing in it is wrong, and the levers are a design step for the owner to call.
2. **Nothing has been heard on the unit.** The renders run the DSP's own code, but DDR timing, overruns and the codec
   are not in the simulator.
3. **The compressor always runs**, at every setting and in every project, so a fault would be heard from start-up.
   None was found in the simulator (e, g).
4. **One set of knobs for three bands.** The quieter bands are squeezed less (in the LIGHTHOUSE-like render: low
   −11.6, mid −9.9, high −4.8 dB mean against stock's −12.9 full-band [verified, `renders.json`]), so at the same
   knobs CMP0 is brighter than stock. **Its loudness at the same knobs depends on the material** [verified, section
   4]: on drums and the mix −3.1 to +2.1 dB RMS against stock, peaks up to +2.8 dB; on sustained or tonal sounds
   (pads, chords, bass) louder, **up to about +5 dB RMS and +8 dB peaks** at heavy settings with high MUP, and about
   +4 dB RMS (+5 dB peaks) at the new-kit defaults with MIX 127. The ceiling does not change: both stop at the
   master's 0.693 (−3.19 dBFS). (An earlier version of this note and of FLASH-CMP0.md said "about as loud, or up to
   3 dB quieter, peaks 0.8 to 2.4 dB higher": that came from the mix and drums renders only.) **A design choice for
   the owner, not built:** trim the summed makeup so that heavy settings match stock's loudness. A fixed trim
   (−4.8 dB when all three bands compress) would make light settings, where one band compresses, quieter than
   stock; a trim that follows how many bands are compressing is more work and more DSP time. Per-band MUP
   (CMP1-CMP3) also lets the owner set it by ear.
5. **SCF does nothing.** An old kit's SCF (default 80, a 119 Hz high-pass in the stock detector) no longer filters
   its detector; with three bands the low band detects itself.
6. **The stock GR bar shows the deepest band** (the most reduction of the three); Overbridge's GR value is the same
   number. Half-words 8/9/10 carry the bands for CMP1's band pages; nothing reads them on GT01's main OS [verified
   in T6.4 2.3]. They overlap dnfw's 1.11 Waverider reply use (soft overlap, T6.4 6.1 #12).
7. **The layout check sees the flanger state as FTD1's fixed FILL** (`0x8400` bytes); it cannot read the flanger
   state's size in the SHARC build (its C guard is a no-op under selcc). The Mac-side size is 33,056 bytes of
   33,792 (`verify_flg.py`). The tape delay's state lives in DDR, outside the check. Adding a size word to
   `flg_glue.c` would change FTD1's image, so it was left [from the confirm pass].
8. **Kits saved on SE01** (if the owner flashed it) load GT01's delay page here: MODE → X, CNTR → HPF, BW → LPF, AGE
   ignored [inferred from SE01's rows, which kept each knob's k].
9. **The listening page** (`out/T6.3/CMP0/ab/index.html`) now records both image hashes (in `renders.json` and
   at the foot of the page); `render_cmp.py` and `verify_cmp.py` refuse an image whose hash does not match
   `build.json`.
10. **The out/T3.11 workbench A/B page** still has the 3-band renders of the committed mbcomp; `cd fx && make
    renders && make stock` would refresh them. Cosmetic: `make_ab.py` still skips the LSTN rows, which no longer
    exist.
